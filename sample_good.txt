Space is silent. No sound travels through the empty dark between the stars. Yet it is full of light, heat, and invisible waves. Every star you see is a hot ball of gas, and many are far bigger than our sun. Light from the nearest star takes more than four years to reach us. That means you are looking at the past. Planets circle those stars, and some may have oceans. Scientists use powerful telescopes to search for them. One day, we may find a world much like ours.
